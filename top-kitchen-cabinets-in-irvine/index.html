Start with how your kitchen needs to work

Top kitchen cabinets in Irvine should suit the way you use your kitchen, not just the style you admire in a photo. Before looking at finishes, notice what’s hard to reach, where items pile up, and which cabinets feel awkward to use. Think about what you’d like to keep, what could be stored differently, and whether your plans include changes to counters, flooring, or tile.

Take a few photos of the kitchen and jot down the changes you hope to make. If you’re deciding between new cabinets and refacing, note what you like about the existing layout and what you want to change. That distinction helps you explain whether your priority is a fresh appearance, improved storage, a broader remodel, or some combination.

Compare cabinet options and prepare

Cabinet replacement and refacing aren’t the same project, so ask what each option would involve for your kitchen. A contractor can review the room, discuss the work you have in mind, and help you understand how cabinets relate to other finishes. Be clear about what you want to keep and what you’d like changed before making a selection.

A useful preparation list:
- Photograph the full kitchen and areas that need attention.
- Make a note of storage problems and items you use often.
- Gather examples of cabinet styles or finishes you like.
- List other planned work, including counters, flooring, or tile.
- Ask what information is needed to discuss replacement or refacing.

One mistake is choosing cabinet color before thinking about the whole room. Another is treating a cabinet update as separate from planned countertop or flooring work. If you’re unsure whether the existing cabinets are suitable for refacing or you want to change how the kitchen functions, call a remodeling professional before settling on a plan.
